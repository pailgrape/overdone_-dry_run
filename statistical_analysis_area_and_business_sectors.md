# Statistical Analysis Area and Business Sectors

## Area of analysis

The statistical analysis in this project is best described as:

- quantitative decision support
- descriptive and inferential analytics
- risk and variability analysis
- performance monitoring and forecasting

It focuses on understanding uncertainty through metrics such as:

- mean
- median
- standard deviation
- confidence intervals
- simulated distributions

In practical terms, this type of analysis answers questions like:

- What is the typical value?
- How much variation is there?
- How confident are we in the estimate?
- What could happen under uncertainty?

## Short summary

The analysis is a general statistical method used to understand patterns, measure variability, estimate uncertainty, and support evidence-based decisions. It is widely used in healthcare, business, finance, operations, and public policy wherever decisions must be made with incomplete or noisy information.
